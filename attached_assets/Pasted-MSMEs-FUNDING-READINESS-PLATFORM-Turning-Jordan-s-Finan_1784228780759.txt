MSMEs FUNDING READINESS PLATFORM
Turning Jordan's Financially Invisible SMEs into Fundable Businesses
Abstract:
We are building a digital twin platform for Jordanian SMEs. It creates a live financial model of a business from its real data: transactions, expenses, employees, assets, and liabilities, aggregated manually or automatically from JoPACC open banking APIs and Fotara API, then lets the owner simulate financial decisions before making them. Should the business take out a loan, and would it strengthen its finances or strain them? How would going green affect its loan eligibility? What happens to cash flow if it hires someone new or raises salaries? Every change ripples across a unified dashboard in real time. The platform also generates credit and green taxonomy scores under CBJ's green taxonomy framework (February 2026), matching MSMEs to the financing products they actually qualify for. 

1  THE PROBLEM

Jordan's MSMEs Are Viable But Financially Invisible
Jordan's MSME sector represents over 99.5% of all registered businesses and provides over 60% of the total workforce. Yet despite this scale, MSMEs are systematically excluded from formal finance. The numbers make this stark:

3  THE SOLUTION

The Platform: How It Works 
The platform is built around a digital twin, a live financial model constructed from the MSME's actual data. The owner can simulate decisions and see financial outcomes before committing. The platform also generates a credit readiness score and a green taxonomy score, connecting MSMEs to the right financing products.  


Step 1: Build the Financial Profile
The MSME connects their financial data through whichever channel fits their reality:
Cliq API integration: automatically imports digital transaction history for businesses already using Cliq payments, using open banking APIs.
JoPACC open banking API: pulls existing loan status and credit history to complete the picture.
JoFotara integration: automatically pulls invoice and billing data directly through the JoFotara API, structured and ready for analysis, managed by the Income and Sales Tax Department. 
Manual input: for cash-based businesses, guided entry with pattern-checking.

The profile also includes asset disclosure. The MSME documents their movable assets: 
Equipment and machinery
Inventory and stock
Vehicles
Any other business assets

Why Asset Disclosure Matters
Jordan has a collateral registry, but banks have been hesitant to use it because MSMEs do not always document what they own. Our platform changes this by making asset disclosure a natural, rewarded part of building a profile, not a regulatory burden. The more a MSME discloses, the more accurate and favorable their score becomes. 


We are aware that many Jordanian MSMEs are cautious about disclosing assets due to concerns around income tax (ضريبة الدخل) and regulatory obligations. Our platform handles this carefully. Asset disclosure is framed through the lens of financial benefit, not regulatory exposure. The simulation shows the SME owner directly how disclosure improves their score and what financing it unlocks, letting them make an informed decision. For tax-related questions the platform surfaces helpful guidance and where relevant refers to a qualified accountant, rather than acting as a tax authority.

The Credit Readiness Score is built directly from the actual lending criteria of Jordanian banks, specifically Jordan Kuwait Bank, scoring an MSME across six categories totaling 100 points.
The Green Taxonomy Score is aligned with CBJ's green taxonomy framework (February 2026), reflecting its core environmental objectives: climate change mitigation, resource efficiency, and pollution prevention.

The scoring model distinguishes between three financing paths:

Green lending: scored against CBJ green taxonomy classification thresholds


Step 3: Simulate the Path Forward
The simulation is the core of the platform. The MSME owner models real financial decisions: hiring, expansion, seasonal changes, loan affordability, green upgrades. The scores update based on the decisions made, giving the owner a clear picture of what their business looks like under any scenario. 
Cash Flow and Liquidity:
When will the business run out of cash at the current burn rate?
What happens to cash flow if a major client pays 60 or 90 days late?
Can the business afford a new hire, and when does it pay back?
What is the impact of buying new equipment now versus later?

Does the business qualify for a green loan under CBJ's green taxonomy framework (February 2026), and at what rate?
How much does the interest rate drop if green sustainability targets are met?

Our Approach 
We do not penalize MSMEs for being informal. We show them exactly what they gain by becoming more compliant; bigger loans, better rates, more opportunities. Every step toward financial transparency is rewarded visibly.







4  GREEN FINANCE & THE TAXONOMY

Why Green Applies to Every MSME, Not Just Manufacturers
Jordan's green taxonomy framework applies to how any business operates, regardless of sector. A restaurant that reduces food waste and sources locally is greener than one that does not. A salon using eco-friendly products scores higher than one that does not. A retailer switching to LED lighting and an electric delivery vehicle improves their taxonomy classification.
Green compliance is not an environmental obligation, it is a financial opportunity. Green-classified businesses in Jordan access loan products with significantly better terms. Our simulation makes this tangible by showing exactly which green changes unlock which financing products, calculated to the specific interest rate difference.
Green lending products available through Jordan Kuwait Bank and CBJ-backed programs include:
Energy Efficiency Loans: up to 4,000,000 JOD at 2.5%–3.5% (outside Amman) or 3%–4% (within Amman), tenure up to 10 years
Renewable Energy Loans: same terms, specifically for solar and wind energy projects
These rates are dramatically lower than conventional SME financing rates (typically 9%–10.5%), giving green-compliant SMEs a concrete financial incentive that our platform quantifies clearly.
