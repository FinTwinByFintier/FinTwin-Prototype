Compare the simulated scenario against the current (real) state before committing to any decision. Step 3: Simulate the Path Forward The simulation is the core of the platform. The MSME owner models real financial decisions: hiring, expansion, seasonal changes, loan affordability, green upgrades. The scores update based on the decisions made, giving the owner a clear picture of what their business looks like under any scenario. Cash Flow and Liquidity: When will the business run out of cash at the current burn rate? What happens to cash flow if a major client pays 60 or 90 days late? Can the business afford a new hire, and when does it pay back? What is the impact of buying new equipment now versus later? Seasonality and Shocks: What if sales drop 20% next quarter? What if energy costs or rent increase by 20%? Financing: Can the business service a loan of X JOD, and what is the safe maximum? How does a new loan's monthly repayment affect cash flow? What financing can equipment or inventory unlock as collateral? Green Finance: If solar panels are installed, what is the payback period versus energy savings? Does the business qualify for a green loan under CBJ's green taxonomy framework (February 2026), and at what rate? How much does the interest rate drop if green sustainability targets are met?
