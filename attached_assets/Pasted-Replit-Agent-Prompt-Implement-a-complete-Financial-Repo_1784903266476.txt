Replit Agent Prompt

Implement a complete Financial Report PDF Export feature for the FinTwin platform.

Objective

When the user clicks "Export Report" on the Dashboard, generate and download a professionally formatted PDF containing:

Income Statement
Cash Flow Statement

Do NOT generate a Balance Sheet because our platform does not currently have sufficient asset information to produce an accurate one.

The layout should resemble a professional accounting statement similar to the attached reference document, with a centered company title, report title, reporting period, clean tables, aligned monetary values, section headers, and totals.

Data Sources

Do NOT request any new information from the user.

Use only existing backend APIs and already synchronized Open Banking data.

Each transaction contains:

Do NOT generate a Balance Sheet.

Income Statement

Generate an Income Statement using transaction data.

Revenue

Revenue equals the sum of all transactions where

direction == "credit"

Formula

Revenue = Σ Credits
Operating Expenses

Operating Expenses equal the sum of all transactions where

direction == "debit"

Formula

Operating Expenses = Σ Debits
Operating Income
Operating Income = Revenue − Operating Expenses

Since we do NOT have reliable accounting data for:

depreciation
taxes
interest expense
cost of goods sold

Do NOT fabricate them.

Instead display

Other Expenses ............ N/A
Taxes ..................... N/A
Net Income

Because no additional deductions exist,

Net Income = Operating Income

The final Income Statement should look similar to

Cash Flow Statement

Generate a simplified Operating Cash Flow Statement.

Cash Inflows

Sum all Credit transactions.

Cash Inflows = Σ Credits
Cash Outflows

Sum all Debit transactions.

Cash Outflows = Σ Debits
Net Cash Flow
Net Cash Flow = Cash Inflows − Cash Outflows

Display

Business Name

CASH FLOW STATEMENT

Reporting Period

--------------------------------------------

Cash Inflows               12,500.00 JOD

Cash Outflows              (8,450.00 JOD)

--------------------------------------------

NET CASH FLOW               4,050.00 JOD

Negative values should appear inside parentheses.
